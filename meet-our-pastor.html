Meet Our Pastor

Pastor Jacob Moreno

Pastor Jacob Moreno serves as pastor of the Kona and Kona Hispanic Seventh-day Adventist Churches, where he shares God’s love in both English and Spanish.

A devoted husband and father of three, Pastor Moreno brings more than 15 years of global missionary experience and a passion for connecting with people through faith, service, and community. He enjoys triathlons, leads a unique bike ministry, and embraces the richness of Hawaiian culture and food. His heart for Christ and others shines through in his ministry, inspiring all ages to grow in their walk with God.

Jesus. Aloha. ʻOhana.
A Christ-centered church family in Kona, Hawaiʻi.
